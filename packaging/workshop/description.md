# 脑雾尖塔 BrainFog

一场发生在脑内的灾难。你还能操作，但认不出任何东西。

- **卡牌失认**：卡牌要打出/升级过一次才能认出来；奖励与选牌界面只显示稀有度
- **文字失读**：事件、菜单、提示、卡面与对话全部乱码，每次启动游戏重新随机（图鉴与设置除外）
- **记忆失真**：生命与金币停留在上次休息时；战斗血条不显示真实血量
- **世界模糊**：敌人只剩呼吸方框、名字隐藏、意图仅战斗首回合显示；遗物不可见、药水只剩轮廓、地图迷雾、Boss 信息隐藏

依赖 STS2 v0.111.0 与 RitsuLib 0.6.2；仅单人；卸载即恢复原版。

Github链接：https://github.com/yM7-1/BrainFog

# BrainFog

A disaster inside your own mind. You can still play — you just can't recognize anything.

- **Card agnosia**: cards stay unrecognizable until played or upgraded once; rewards and selection screens show rarity only
- **Text alexia**: events, menus, prompts, card text and dialogue are garbled, re-rolled on every launch (compendium and settings stay readable)
- **Faulty memory**: HP and gold are frozen at your last rest; the combat health bar hides true HP
- **Blurred world**: enemies are breathing boxes with hidden names and intents only on the first combat round; relics invisible, potions outlined, map fogged, boss info hidden

Requires STS2 v0.111.0 + RitsuLib 0.6.2. Single-player only; uninstall restores everything.

GitHub: https://github.com/yM7-1/BrainFog
